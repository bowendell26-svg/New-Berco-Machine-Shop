Core machining services

From a single worn bushing to a full engine rebuild, we machine parts to the size your equipment needs.

Engine Reboring & Cylinder Sleeving

Restore worn cylinder bores to the right size, or fit new sleeves, so compression and oil control come back.

Cylinder Head Resurfacing & Valve Work

Flat head surfaces, valve seat cutting, and valve grinding for a tight seal and a reliable head gasket joint.

Crankshaft Grinding & Line Boring

Journal grinding to undersize and line boring of engine blocks for true alignment and correct bearing clearance.

Lathe & Milling Precision Machining

Turning, facing, threading, slotting, and milling of steel, brass, and aluminum to your drawing or sample part.

Custom Bushing, Shaft & Gear Fabrication

Made-to-measure replacements for parts that are obsolete, imported, or too slow to order.

Heavy Welding & Structural Repair

Crack repair, build-up of worn surfaces, and frame or bracket work for trucks, tractors, and heavy equipment.
